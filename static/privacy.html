Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

security 1. Data Protection & Security

At FinanceFit AI, we prioritize protecting your financial and personal data. All user credentials and passwords are encrypted on our servers using PBKDF2 (Password-Based Key Derivation Function 2) with SHA-256 and custom unique salts.

analytics 2. Data Collection and Usage

We collect essential information to provide financial analysis:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, password hashes, and referral code logs.
  • Financial Metrics: Anonymous company metrics (revenue, cash flow, debt ratio) when using the ML predictors.
  • Usage Data: Session records, connected developer API keys, and notification histories.

vpn_key 3. Session Management

To safeguard your wealth profile, FinanceFit AI enforces single-session verification checks. When you log in, older active sessions are automatically terminated to prevent concurrent unauthorized access. Session tokens are checked server-side for validation.

share 4. Referrals Policy

If you use our "Invite a Friend" referral feature, we record the referee email and your referral code in our secure database to check invitation eligibility and reward delivery. We do not sell or lease this information to third-party advertisers.

mail 5. Contact Compliance

If you have questions about this policy or your data rights, please contact our security team at security@financefit.ai.
